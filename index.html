FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is NoBuyDays?

NoBuyDays is a colorful no-spend calendar app for iPhone. It helps you track no-buy days, purchases, sales, and the money you chose not to spend.

How does the NoBuyDays app work?

Log each day as no spend, planned buy, impulse buy, or sold. NoBuyDays turns those entries into a color-coded monthly calendar and a shareable yearly recap.

What counts as a no-buy day?

You decide. Most people exclude rent, bills, groceries, and planned essentials, then track the discretionary purchases they want to reduce.

Can I log purchases and sales?

Yes. Planned buys, impulse buys, and sold items each keep their own amount so your month remains easy to read.

Can I fill future days?

No. Future dates stay untouched. NoBuyDays is a record of what actually happened, not a promise you have to defend.

Is my calendar private?

Yes. Sharing is intentional. A Sign in with Apple account is only required when you choose to create a profile or use community features.

Will NoBuyDays be available on Android?

NoBuyDays is being built for iPhone first. Android is not part of the initial launch.
