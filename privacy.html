NoBuyDays

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

NoBuyDays is designed to keep your daily calendar private on your iPhone. This policy explains what is stored locally and what is shared when you choose to use community features.

Data stored on your device

Your daily entries, purchase amounts, sale amounts, reminder preferences, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to our servers unless you sign in and use community progress features.

Account and community data

If you sign in with Apple, we process your account identifier, chosen username, optional profile photo, friendships, groups, messages, shared images, and the calendar status needed to show community progress. Apple may provide a relay email depending on your Sign in with Apple choices.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. NoBuyDays receives entitlement information but does not receive or store your payment card details.

Notifications and photos

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. Photo access is used only when you choose a profile photo or send an image. Camera access is used only for QR-code scanning.

Deletion

You can delete your community account from the profile screen. This removes the account and associated community data. Local calendar data can be cleared separately in Settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the project page at github.com/thetotobalgo/nobuydays.
